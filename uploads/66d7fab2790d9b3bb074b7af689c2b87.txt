Google Interview Prep · 21-Day Plan · Page 9
Final Tips & Strategy
Daily Routine 1 hr theory in the morning · 2.5 hrs LeetCode practice · 1 hr review solutions. Repeat every day
with the day's specific focus.
Pattern Over
Volume
150 problems done with reflection beats 400 done by copy-pasting solutions. Google tests
pattern recognition, not memorization.
Behavioral is Not
Optional
Google's hiring committee scores Googleyness as a separate signal. Day 19 STAR stories
need the same prep rigor as Day 9 DP.
Gym: Cognitive
Boost
3 hours of exercise improves memory consolidation by up to 20%. Schedule gym in the
morning to prime your brain for the study blocks.
Project Work Use project time to apply what you've learned — build a small full-stack app or contribute to
open source. Demonstrates initiative in interviews.
Mock Interviews Days 7, 14, and 21 are dedicated mock days. Use Pramp and Interviewing.io. Talk through
your approach aloud every time — silence kills in Google interviews.
